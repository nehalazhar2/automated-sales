## Overview

Operating in a regulated environment where compliance is not optional. We worked with the team to introduce AI into the sales and operational workflow in a way that helps the team — without creating new compliance risk.

## What we worked on

- Narrow AI agents scoped to specific tasks (triage, summary, drafting)
- Call transcription with controlled storage and access patterns
- Reporting and audit trails so every AI-assisted action is traceable
- Process design that keeps a human in the loop wherever a decision affects a customer
