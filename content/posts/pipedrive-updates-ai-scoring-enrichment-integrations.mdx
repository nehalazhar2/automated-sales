Pipedrive has rolled out a set of product changes and partnerships that target the biggest practical pain point for sales teams: too much administrative work and too little time selling. This article synthesizes new features—custom scoring, in-app data enrichment, AI beta tools, a Model Context Protocol (MCP) connector in Claude’s marketplace—and strategic moves like the Outfunnel acquisition, and explains what they mean for revenue teams.

## Overview

Across recent product and newsroom announcements, Pipedrive focused on three visible aims: reduce manual data entry, surface higher-value work, and make CRM data usable inside the AI tools people already use. The company introduced built-in scoring and enrichment within the Pulse toolkit, opened a beta channel for AI workflows, published an MCP connector in Claude’s marketplace to let large language models read and update CRM records, and brought Outfunnel’s real‑time sales–marketing sync into the platform through acquisition. These updates are practical responses to the CRM Trends Report 2026 findings that sales teams are spending far more time on logging activities than on selling.

## Key Highlights

- Custom deal scoring (Pulse): create rules that automatically rank opportunities based on deal fields, activity, and business logic so teams prioritize higher-probability deals.
- Data Enrichment (Pulse): one-click enrichment for organization and person records pulls publicly available company size, revenue, phone, location and LinkedIn details; only empty fields are suggested and changes require confirmation.
- AI beta program: opt-in company-wide access to experimental features such as email summarization, suggested replies, deal summaries and an AI Sales Assistant for conversational CRM queries.
- MCP Connector in Claude’s marketplace: a packaged connector that lets Anthropic’s Claude surface and act on Pipedrive data (reads and writes), now with support for Leads and an easier connection flow than manual MCP configuration. See the announcement for details [on Pipedrive.com](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/newsroom/pipedrive-mcp-connector-is-now-available-in-claudes-official-marketplace).
- Outfunnel acquisition: Pipedrive acquired the top-rated marketplace app for sales–marketing sync, planning to embed real‑time marketing integrations natively rather than relying on a third-party add-on.
- Context: Pipedrive’s CRM Trends Report 2026 shows administrative load is the primary blocker for selling productivity—these features are designed to address that gap.

## Business Impact

These updates reduce friction at three operational layers.

First, automation and enrichment cut the time reps spend hunting for basic contact or company data and manual logging. That directly addresses the report’s finding that many reps act as data-entry clerks rather than sellers.

Second, custom scoring focuses attention on deals that match your ICP and current signals, improving win-rate potential by reallocating limited selling hours to higher-value opportunities. Scores update in real time as deal data changes, keeping prioritization relevant without added overhead.

Third, tighter integrations—Outfunnel’s sync becoming part of Pipedrive and the MCP connector inside Claude—shrink tool-switching costs. Marketing and sales teams get a shared, consistent source of truth, and AI workflows can draw on live CRM context to summarize, suggest, and act without manual exports or fragile scripts.

## Implementation & Best Practices

Adopting these capabilities requires a measured, governance-driven approach. Key recommendations:

**1. Start small with scoring.** Build an initial score using 2–4 high‑value signals (e.g., deal stage, estimated value, recent activity). Test on a sample pipeline, verify that high-scoring deals align with closed-won history, then iterate. Keep rules transparent so reps understand why opportunities are prioritized.

**2. Use enrichment to fill gaps, not overwrite judgement.** Data Enrichment proposes values only for empty fields and asks for confirmation before changes. Validate enriched fields in a pilot cohort, then enable broader usage. Enrichment works best when person records include a work email or linked organization with a website or LinkedIn profile.

**3. Control AI beta access centrally.** The beta program grants company-wide access after a single opt-in; use account settings to restrict or enable features by policy. Run a small internal pilot to measure accuracy and ROI for email summaries, suggested replies, and the AI Sales Assistant before rolling out team-wide.

**4. Treat MCP integrations as governed automation.** The Claude MCP connector simplifies connections to Anthropic surfaces, but it still respects user permissions. Review who can connect tools, log usage, and provide training on what types of read/write actions are allowed. For technical teams, consult the Pipedrive developer resources for guidance on MCP usage and permissions: [developers.pipedrive.com](https://developers.pipedrive.com).

**5. Plan your marketing-sync migration.** Outfunnel will be transitioned into the core product over months with no immediate disruption. Map current sync rules and audience segments so you can recreate or improve them natively when the rebuilt capability becomes available.

**6. Measure outcomes.** Track metrics that matter: time spent on admin tasks, percent of activities logged automatically, changes in response times, lead-to-opportunity conversion, and win rate for high-scoring deals. Use automation and integration reports to quantify reductions in context switching.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which plans include custom scoring and data enrichment?

Custom scoring and Data Enrichment are available on Premium and Ultimate tiers. The beta program is available starting on Premium (or Professional for some legacy plans). If you need help confirming what your account has access to, visit your account settings or contact support via [Pipedrive help](/pipedrive-help/).

### Will these features overwrite or expose private data?

Data Enrichment only fills empty fields and requires user confirmation before committing changes; existing values are preserved. Enrichment sources publicly available data and the integration is designed with compliance controls. MCP and AI connectors respect Pipedrive’s permission model—applications can only access or update records the connecting user is allowed to see or edit. Review your company’s beta and connector settings before enabling any external tool.
