## What is a Pipedrive consultant?

A Pipedrive consultant functions as a CRM expert capable of implementing systems and training teams. However, the most effective consultants extend beyond technical knowledge to serve as sales strategists. Since Pipedrive was designed by sales professionals for sales professionals, consultants should understand both the software and sales processes.

Our approach involves experts who combine software implementation skills with sales leadership experience across various industries and business stages. We collaborate with partners to map current sales processes, then recommend Pipedrive configurations that work technically while improving sales outcomes. This includes identifying opportunities to integrate systems, automate workflows, and refine processes that drive revenue growth.

## Why is this needed?

Pipedrive's intuitive, visual design enables rapid adoption among sales teams. However, initial setup becomes complex when accounting for existing systems and processes. Strategic integrations with other platforms can significantly enhance efficiency and results while reducing manual work.

## What can Pipedrive consultants integrate?

While Pipedrive excels as a sales-focused CRM, it requires complementary systems for complete functionality. Our consultants integrate marketing platforms, email services, contracting software, and invoicing systems to create a centralized data hub.

For example, we build integrations that trigger email campaigns to prospect segments based on Pipedrive activity changes, using platforms like Mailchimp or [ActiveCampaign](/active-campaign-consultants/). Workflows automate activities and alerts, preventing leads from slipping through the cracks and saving teams hours of weekly administrative time.

## Experience

Consultants should deliver measurable value through strategic recommendations focused on time savings and revenue generation. A good consultancy will be able to point to real projects, real outcomes and real client feedback.

## Summary

When selecting a [Pipedrive consultant](/pipedrive-consultant/), consider:

1. Can they showcase previous work examples?
2. Is client feedback available?
3. What project sizes do they typically handle?
4. What is their implementation approach?
5. Do they provide ongoing support?
6. Have they addressed your specific challenges?
