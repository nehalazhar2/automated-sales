There are multiple approaches to automatically populate your CRM with leads, with selection depending on your specific CRM, integration tools, lead volume, and budget considerations.

## Built-in CRM features

Pipedrive offers native webforms that can be embedded directly on your website. When visitors complete these forms, data syncs automatically into the CRM. You can customize fields to include standard information like name and email, plus any custom fields needed for additional data collection. This approach works well for new websites or those willing to add new functionality.

## Form plugin integrations

If you already have an existing website using form tools, popular options like WPForms or Gravity Forms have developed Pipedrive plugins for form submission imports. Different plugins offer varying capabilities — some create only Contacts while others can generate Contacts, Organizations, and Deals. Research is essential before selecting a plugin.

## Zapier integration

[Zapier](/zapier-consultants/) connects thousands of web applications and is valuable for nearly all Pipedrive implementation projects. It can integrate website forms and create granular workflows: generating Contacts, Organizations, and Deals; moving Deals through pipeline stages; and automatically creating Activities for sales follow-ups. This automation saves team time while populating Pipedrive with new leads.

## Outfunnel

Originally designed to bridge Pipedrive and Mailchimp, Outfunnel now offers Zapier-like functionality for creating leads from webforms. Its distinguishing feature is web tracking capability — monitoring visitor journeys across your site and displaying which pages prospects visited and time spent on each, informing more targeted follow-up strategies.
