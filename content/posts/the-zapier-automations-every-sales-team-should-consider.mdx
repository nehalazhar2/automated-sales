Most sales teams use 5% of what Zapier (or Make, or Pipedrive workflows) can do. The reason is usually not capability — it is that nobody has scoped the right automations against the actual sales process. Here are the seven we deploy on almost every engagement.

## 1. Web-form to CRM, with routing

The hello-world of sales automation, but most teams still get it wrong. Get this right and the rest gets easier:

- Validate the email is real and not a personal address (if you sell B2B)
- Enrich with a firmographic lookup
- Route based on territory, deal size or product
- Assign to the right owner, with an SLA timer
- Notify the rep in Slack, not just email

## 2. Stalled deal nudge

Anything sitting in a stage longer than the average cycle time triggers a Slack ping to the owner and an entry on the manager's "to review" view. Not a passive Pipedrive "rotting" indicator — an active poke.

## 3. Won-deal handoff

When a deal moves to Won, fire a structured handoff to onboarding: who, what they bought, who sold it, key promises made, and links to call recordings. This single automation saves more inter-team friction than any other.

## 4. Lost-deal feedback loop

When a deal moves to Lost, prompt the rep for a structured reason (drop-down, not free text) and surface trends monthly. Without this, "lost to price" stays the default lie.

## 5. Activity sync

Calls, calendar invites and email replies sync to the right contact and deal automatically. If reps are logging activity manually, they are paying a tax that compounds.

## 6. Data hygiene patrol

Daily: find contacts missing key fields, deals missing close dates, deals without next-actions. Surface to the owner with a one-click fix link.

## 7. Reporting feeder

Push CRM events to Sheets or BigQuery on a schedule so reporting tooling is never blocked on the Pipedrive API.

## What we usually do not automate

A few things people ask for that we usually push back on:

- Auto-creating tasks for every email reply (creates noise)
- AI-generated follow-up emails sent without human review (brand risk)
- Bulk reassignment scripts run automatically (high blast radius)
